## Paired design

Use `TR02-CTRL-002` (MountainCar-v0) with the vendored WeightBench evaluator and a `2 → 96 → 96 → 3` ReLU policy. Generate five checkpoints with independent seed settings in the same behavior-cloning and pruning recipe. Each seed generates one training instance and its own start pools. This design samples one generator family with the same training task, architecture, expert, and evaluation rules. It has one Codex replicate per fixture and form, so it cannot separately estimate variability over repeated editor sessions on a fixed input.

The generator uses 60,000 independently sampled position-velocity training states, 40 epochs of expert imitation, batch size 512, AdamW with learning rate `0.001` and zero weight decay, and a cosine schedule. Six first-layer and four second-layer hidden units are structurally masked throughout training. The resulting trained policy becomes the starting checkpoint directly. The generator samples a 24,000-state start pool, rolls it out with the starting checkpoint, and partitions success and failure into disjoint training and held-out regions, with a separate failure demonstration. Its two held-out regions each have 100 starts. Each paired run uses eight seeded development starts per region sampled without replacement from its training regions. ## Arms and budget

Each cell has two scored development candidate slots, including the unchanged checkpoint. One slot reserves 3,200 network input rows, based on 16 starts and a 200-step horizon. Each method has a 6,400-row development ceiling. Actual rollout rows can be lower because trajectories terminate early and proposals may leave slots unused. Run one separate held-out evaluation on the selected checkpoint with a 40,000-row ceiling. The objective is the unweighted mean of failure-start success and normal-start success. The latter measures retention. Each successful rollout reaches position at least `0.5` with nonnegative velocity by step 200.

All arms use one broker and the four common operations `scale_neuron`, `zero_neuron`, `shift_bias`, and `rank_one`. Each recipe can contain up to 16 operations and change up to 4% of checkpoint entries. Shared bounds are scales in `[0.25, 4]`, bias shifts in `[-4, 4]`, rank-one multipliers in `[-4, 4]`, and rank-one vector entries in `[-1, 1]`. Search and the analytic controller rank scored candidates by development objective, then fewer changed entries, then earlier candidate ID. Codex explicitly selects a scored candidate from its development history. All methods commit selection before held-out evaluation. Codex sees the task specification, checkpoint, edit rules, and broker development feedback. Held-out starts and outcomes remain with the trusted evaluator. The model's accepted output length also limits which edit recipes it can communicate, especially for dense rank-one vectors.

1. `no_edit` selects the unchanged checkpoint.
2. `compound_random` draws one ordered recipe with length uniform from 4 through 16 inclusive and independently sampled edit atoms.
3. `compound_greedy` starts with a randomly oriented signed coordinate block of length uniform from 4 through 16 inclusive. Its general search can compare a reflected block and build on accepted coordinates when more slots are available. This budget provides one scored block and therefore no adaptive coordinate update.
4. `compound_es` starts a `(1 + 4)` evolutionary search with independent long recipes from the empty parent, with the same genesis distribution as `compound_random` and a separate random stream. Nonempty parents use add, replace, or delete mutations with one to four edits per child. The panel budget permits one scored offspring and therefore no across-generation adaptation.
5. `velocity_controller` compiles a task-aware policy prior using the sign of velocity. It chooses left for negative velocity, right for positive velocity, and left at exactly zero by the action tie rule. The fixture's imitation teacher also pushes right for positions above `-0.45`; the analytic control uses velocity alone. Pick the first two structurally masked units by index in each hidden layer, wire the positive and negative velocity channels through them, and replace the three output rows and biases to implement logits `[ReLU(-z), 0, ReLU(z)]` for normalized velocity `z`. Use bounded rank-one updates and bias shifts in a single recipe of at most nine operations. Compare the recipe to the unchanged checkpoint through the development broker. Construction uses checkpoint coefficients and the known input/action semantics without rollouts. This arm measures a task-specific compiled prior.
6. `codex` receives the full checkpoint and links a proposal to a choice among the scored candidates. The configured requested model is `gpt-6.2-sol`, effort `low`, via Codex CLI `0.169.0` with native ChatGPT login and its built-in provider. The pinned Direct-mode catalog is derived from the source descriptor with only the `tool_mode` field changed. Both SHA-256 values are in the frozen plan. Use the same descriptor and derived catalog throughout. Keep the existing native account cache and environment locations intact and retain the descriptor privately.

Compound search draws from streams `SeedSequence([run_seed, 3, i])` with indices `0`, `1`, and `2` for random, greedy, and ES. The atomic family is uniform over the four primitives. An eligible layer and neuron coordinate are uniformly sampled within that family. Magnitudes for scale are drawn from `{0.5, 0.8, 1.25, 2}` and magnitudes for shifts and rank-one multipliers from `{-1, -0.25, 0.25, 1}`. Greedy uses an opposite pair with matched reciprocal scale or matched absolute magnitude, with one-hot rank-one vectors. Random and ES rank-one vectors have support sizes drawn uniformly from one through `min(4, axis width)` and ones at uniformly sampled distinct positions. A broker rejection before scoring consumes an attempt and no development slot. Each search arm makes at most 12 proposal attempts given the single extra slot. The only reported comparison for each search arm is its performance under this one-scored-proposal budget.

Each Codex cell can start up to two model-bearing `codex exec` attempts. It uses zero harness retries, a 90-second deadline and 128 KiB captured process output per attempt, and acceptance thresholds of 120,000 reported input tokens and 8,192 reported output tokens per attempt. The panel raises the pilot's 2,048-token output acceptance threshold prospectively to give long multi-operation recipes room for serialization. If all 20 model-bearing attempts complete and pass the threshold, accepted output totals at most 163,840 reported tokens. Local version and login-status checks are tracked separately from the model-bearing cap. The global upper bound is 20 model-bearing attempts. About 1.8 million input tokens are projected at full execution from the prior pilot's roughly 89,000 reported input tokens per turn. Token checks follow reported usage and the native CLI may perform finite internal transport retries. The subscription charge and internal request count are unavailable. Use the pinned native route throughout the panel. The launch configuration does not use an API billing fallback.

## Outcomes and analysis

- The primary contrast is Codex minus the analytic velocity controller on the held-out objective. Compute the difference in each paired form, average the two form differences within each fixture, then average the five fixture differences with equal weights.
- Prespecified secondary comparisons use the same arithmetic for Codex minus each compound arm and minus the unchanged checkpoint. Show original and transformed results explicitly. Also report original minus transformed within each arm as a performance difference. The design has one transform and one editor draw per form, so those differences combine form and editor variation.
- For one additional search summary, choose a single compound method by largest mean development objective across all fixtures and forms, with ties resolved in the order `compound_random`, `compound_greedy`, `compound_es`. Compare its held-out outcomes with Codex. No held-out score enters method or checkpoint selection.
- Report every planned fixture, form, and arm with run state and reason, its selected candidate and edit, development and held-out objective and region success counts and rates, and development minus held-out objective when present. Retain attempted and rejected proposals, reserved/used rows, and native attempt/usage evidence in the journals. For each arm and form report the available sample count, mean, sample standard deviation, and range as descriptive summaries. Also show the individual fixture and form differences for each contrast when the required rows are present.
- For each planned aggregate contrast, require all five comparable fixture units. Use a paired percentile cluster bootstrap with 10,000 samples of five fixtures with replacement from NumPy `default_rng(20261120)`. Both forms and all needed methods travel together with each resampled fixture. Reselect the development winner within every replicate for the compound search summary. The interval uses the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles with linear interpolation. With five clusters the descriptive intervals are coarse and unstable, especially near a ceiling or when all observed differences are equal. The results concern this generator, task, budget, and editor setup. Secondary comparisons are descriptive and have no multiplicity adjustment.

## Execution, failures, and replay

Stage the exact Git source archive, installed wheel, prospective config and protocol, catalog inputs, expanded experiment configs, and fixture payloads with SHA-256 hashes in a uniquely named private directory. Seal the input manifest before the single authorized launch. Validate all fixture seeds, split hashes, and paired starting checkpoints under the frozen wheel before live execution. Each fixture writes a separate append-only experiment directory. The panel records a single exclusive launch claim. Each cell and all output records are auditable from its raw journal and checkpoint artifacts.

A hard native CLI failure or limit stops all later launches and fixture jobs. Relevant failures include CLI version or login mismatch, process and stream failures, unexpected diagnostics, catalog integrity failures, and reported usage over threshold. Invalid editor proposals fail their cell with zero retries. A structurally rejected broker recipe consumes an attempt with zero evaluation rows and no candidate reservation. A rollout error after reservation consumes its slot and is a candidate-level failure. Report any resulting terminal selection in the raw rows, mark the cell invalid for contrasts, and preserve the candidate error. The plan retains all five seeds and all 60 rows. Record failed, incomplete, invalid, and unstarted cells and use explicit missing outcomes. Withhold a planned aggregate estimate and interval if any required fixture unit is missing or mismatched. Preserve completed raw rows and any identifiable partial paired differences. A recorded model command can represent an attempted launch that failed before process creation. For an interrupted harness, journaled attempts and reported usage may give only lower bounds. Retain the complete available attempt and stop record. Any repeat, seed substitution, tuning, or extension requires a separate prospective protocol.

Before releasing or interpreting panel numbers, an independent verifier should check the frozen package and data, reconstruct transforms and edited policies, replay development candidates and held-out selections from the raw fixture data, audit CLI attempt and budget evidence, and recompute the paired analysis. A release can contain verified per-cell result data and exact replay inputs after review for credentials, account metadata, private paths, and sensitive native evidence. A replay of fixed candidate recipes and selected checkpoints needs no native calls. A fresh Codex execution is a separate replication.
